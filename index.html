03 / 05
Weave
AI-Agent Video Editor · Local-First · Python + LLM

A local-first editor that turns a raw 2-hour Zoom recording into a finished, branded video — automatically. It transcribes the recording, cuts dead air, filler words, and false starts, and uses AI to find the strongest narrative hook and reorder for it — every decision auditable and reversible. Renders 4K (a 2-hour video in ~20 min) and exports an MP4 or a Premiere project. In production: 3+ videos/week for a coaching brand, cutting ~6 hours of manual editing per video.
